What to know before you come

Saturday, October 107:30 AM – 1:00 PMOld Escondido Historic District

Sales run from 7:30 until 1:00. Most households pack up by noon, so the best time to come is early. Bring small bills — most sales are cash.

Where do I get a printed map?+

At the top of 6th Ave, from 6:30 to 8:30 AM. They’re free — take one for a neighbour.

Where should I park?+

On the street, and please leave driveways clear. The district is walkable end to end in about twenty minutes.

Is it cash only?+

Bring cash and small bills. Some households take Venmo or Zelle, most don’t.

Does it run in the rain?+

Yes, unless it’s heavy. Check this page Saturday morning — we’ll post here first.
